## What is an Enagic Kangen Water machine?

An Enagic Kangen Water machine is a water ionizer designed to process tap water through filtration and electrolysis. The system uses electrolysis to produce water with different pH characteristics, depending on the selected setting and the source water.

The machines are designed for countertop installation and connect to a water supply. Different models vary in electrode plate configuration, capacity, features, price, and warranty.

Kangen Water machines are manufactured in Japan and sold through Enagic's distributor network.

## How does a Kangen Water machine work?

The process generally involves three stages:

### 1. Filtration

Incoming tap water passes through the machine's filter to reduce certain impurities, depending on the filter design and its rated performance.

### 2. Electrolysis

The filtered water passes through an electrolysis chamber containing electrodes. The electrical process changes the water's pH characteristics.

### 3. Water selection

The user selects a desired setting through the machine's controls. The machine produces water according to that setting and its operating conditions.

The machine does not replace the need to ensure that your source water is safe and suitable for the device.
